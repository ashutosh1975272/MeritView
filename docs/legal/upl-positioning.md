# UPL Positioning Document

## Purpose
Define MeritView's positioning as "decision support, not legal advice" to avoid UPL (Unauthorized Practice of Law) concerns.

## Core Positioning Statement
*MeritView provides AI-powered decision support for contract dispute analysis. It does not provide legal advice, create an attorney-client relationship, or substitute for licensed legal counsel. All analysis is for informational purposes only.*

## Key Distinctions
| MeritView Is | MeritView Is Not |
|---|---|
| Decision support tool | Legal advice |
| Analytical framework | Attorney representation |
| Argument identifier | Legal strategy |
| Information organizer | Legal opinion |
| User-empowerment tool | Lawyer replacement |

## UPL Risk Factors Mitigated
- [ ] No attorney-client relationship established
- [ ] No personalized legal recommendations
- [ ] Clear disclaimers on every output
- [ ] No representation in legal proceedings
- [ ] User retains full decision authority

## Required Disclaimers (from T0.2.15)
1. **Not Legal Advice:** "This analysis is for informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice."
2. **No Attorney-Client Relationship:** "Use of MeritView does not create an attorney-client relationship."
3. **Jurisdictional Limitation:** "Laws vary by jurisdiction. Consult a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction."
4. **AI-Generated Content:** "This analysis was generated by an AI system and should be independently verified."
