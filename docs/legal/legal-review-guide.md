# Legal Review Guide

## Purpose
Guide for engaging a tech lawyer specializing in AI and UPL (Unauthorized Practice of Law) regulation.

## Lawyer Engagement Checklist

### Preparation
- [ ] Research tech lawyers with AI/UPL expertise (T0.2.1)
- [ ] Prepare MeritView value proposition and "decision support, not legal advice" positioning (T0.2.2)
- [ ] Draft disclaimers ready for review (T0.2.4)

### Review Request
- [ ] Send positioning document for written review (T0.2.3)
- [ ] Request list of legally required disclaimers (T0.2.5)
- [ ] Request list of prohibited jurisdictions (T0.2.6)
- [ ] Provide ToS draft for review (T0.2.7)
- [ ] Provide privacy policy draft for review (T0.2.8)

### Implementation
- [ ] Incorporate required disclaimers into opinion delivery plan (T0.2.9)
- [ ] Incorporate prohibited jurisdictions into geo-blocking plan (T0.2.10)
- [ ] Obtain written confirmation to proceed (T0.2.11)
- [ ] Store legal documents in infra/legal/ directory (T0.2.12 ✓)
- [ ] Add legal review checkpoint to CI and release process (T0.2.13)
- [ ] Finalize 4 standard disclaimers list (T0.2.15)

## Legal Review Cadence
- **Quarterly** legal review (T0.2.14)
- Trigger events that may require ad-hoc review:
  - New jurisdiction launch
  - Regulatory change in AI/legal tech
  - User complaint about legal advice perception
  - New feature that changes opinion delivery
